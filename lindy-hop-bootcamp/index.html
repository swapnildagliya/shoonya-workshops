Lindy Hop teacher

Ann-Sofie
Van Enis

An eternal student, Ann-Sofie likes to delve into the history of jazz music and dance, sharing her learning in each class. She introduces everyone to musicality and personal expression in an approachable way. In addition to teaching Lindy Hop and Solo Jazz, she organizes events and projects for Upside Down.

Photo © Leyla Hesna

Lindy Hop teacher

Sep
Vermeersch

Sep is a Ghent-based dancer, active as a teacher, versatile artist, MC, DJ, and artistic director at Upside Down. His excellent reputation reaches far beyond Belgium's borders. Thanks to his great passion for the historical background of swing and jazz dance cultures and an inexhaustible creativity, he knows how to move young and old.
