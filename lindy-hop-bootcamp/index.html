An eternal student, Ann-Sofie likes to delve into the history of jazz music and dance, sharing her learning in each class. She introduces everyone to musicality and personal expression in an approachable way. In addition to teaching Lindy Hop and Solo Jazz, she organizes events and projects for Upside Down.

Sep is a Ghent-based dancer, active as a teacher, versatile artist, MC, DJ, and artistic director at Upside Down. His excellent reputation reaches far beyond Belgium's borders. A 2.5-hour Lindy Hop bootcamp on Wednesday 9 September 2026 (18:45–21:15) at Shoonya Dance Centre, Ghent — to refresh your vocabulary before the new season of dance classes. Taught by Ann-Sofie Van Enis and Sep Vermeersch. Drill swing outs, partnered Charleston, tuck turns, change of places and many more swing dance moves. Intermediate level. €35.

Intermediate — minimum 1 year of Lindy Hop experience, or having completed the Beginners series at Shoonya. The session assumes you already recognise what a swing out and basic Charleston look like. The aim is to refine and deepen those moves, not to introduce them for the first time.

No partner required. The bootcamp runs on a "everybody leads, everybody follows" basis — you'll practice both roles and rotate partners throughout. Come alone, come as a couple, come with a friend — everyone is welcome as an individual.

No one is assigned a fixed lead or follow role for the session. Every participant practices both roles. If you want to stick to one role, that is also fine.

Swing outs, partnered Charleston, tuck turns, change of places and many more swing dance moves — with special attention for a comfortable connection, full body movement, clear rhythms and freedom in improvisation.

Studio Ananta — ground floor — inside Shoonya Dance Centre at Stapelplein 41, 9000 Ghent (Gent), Belgium. Studio Ananta consists of two adjoining studios — Ananta 1 (80m²) and Ananta 2 (60m²) — giving 140 square metres of dance floor for this bootcamp. More at shoonyadance.com.

€35 per person. Covers the full 2.5-hour session. A Shoonya ID is required to register — free to create at shoonyadance.com. Minimum 10 participants for the session to run. Maximum 40.

Comfortable, form-fitting dancewear you can move in. Appropriate dance shoes or socks. Bring a water bottle.
