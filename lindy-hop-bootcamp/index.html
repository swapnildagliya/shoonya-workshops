About this bootcamp

Two and a half hours to make your core Lindy Hop moves feel automatic.

This bootcamp cuts straight to the fundamentals. In a single focused evening, Ann-Sofie Van Enis and Sep Vermeersch guide you through deep repetition of the moves that define Lindy Hop on the social floor: swing outs, partnered Charleston, tuck turns and change of places.

No new choreography. No performance. Just deliberate drilling of the vocabulary you already know — until it stops living in your head and starts living in your body.

FAQ

What is the Lindy Hop bootcamp at Shoonya?

A 2.5-hour intensive session on Wednesday 9 September 2026 (18:45–21:15) at Shoonya Dance Centre, Ghent. Taught by Ann-Sofie Van Enis and Sep Vermeersch, it focuses on drilling the four core moves that define the social floor: swing outs, partnered Charleston, tuck turns and change of places. Intermediate level. €35.

What level do I need for this Lindy Hop bootcamp?

Intermediate — minimum 1 year of Lindy Hop experience, or having completed the Beginners series at Shoonya. The session assumes you already recognise what a swing out and basic Charleston look like. The aim is to refine and deepen those moves, not to introduce them for the first time.

Do I need a dance partner to attend?

No partner required. The bootcamp runs on a "everybody leads, everybody follows" basis — you'll practice both roles and rotate partners throughout. Come alone, come as a couple, come with a friend — everyone is welcome as an individual.

What does "everybody leads, everybody follows" mean?

No one is assigned a fixed lead or follow role for the session. Every participant practices both sides of each move in every drill. Experiencing a movement from both perspectives deepens your understanding and sharpens your execution — whichever role you normally dance.

What moves will we drill?

Four core moves: swing outs (the fundamental Lindy Hop step), partnered Charleston (the signature circling step), tuck turns (an in-and-out arm-connection move) and change of places (a cross-body travelling step). These are the moves that come up every social dance. The goal is muscle memory and musicality.

Where is the bootcamp in Ghent?

Studio Ananta, inside Shoonya Dance Centre at Stapelplein 41, 9000 Ghent (Gent), Belgium. Studio Ananta consists of two adjoining studios — Ananta 1 (80m²) and Ananta 2 (60m²) — giving 140 square metres of dance floor for this bootcamp. More at shoonyadance.com.

How much does it cost and what's included?

€35 per person. This covers the full 2.5-hour session including warm-up, two drilling blocks (swing outs; partnered Charleston, tuck turns and change of places) and a social practice floor. Both lead and follow roles are included — no extras.
