About the intensive

The foundations of
this expressive art form.

An immersive flamenco experience with four in-depth workshops of two hours each. La Liz creates a relaxed, yet focused learning environment to explore technique, fiesta styles and the foundations of this expressive art form. Rather than learning choreography, the emphasis is on developing a deeper understanding of movement, rhythm, musicality, and expression. You are free to join one or several sessions.

Session 01
Wednesday · 26 August · 19:00–21:00

Flamenco
Dance Technique

For anyone with at least one season of flamenco experience

A solid technique creates freedom in dance. In these sessions, we will refine posture and arm movements, practice marcajes, spatial transitions, turns, rhythm, coordination and precision. A perfect refresher after the holidays and for those who've studied flamenco before and want to get back into it. Although each session can be taken independently, attending both technique sessions is highly recommended for deeper physical integration and lasting improvement.

19:00 – 21:00 €33 / session
Session 02
Thursday · 27 August · 19:00–21:00

Por Fiesta
Bulerías

For anyone with at least one season of flamenco experience

Discover the playful and social side of flamenco. Working with simple foundations and characteristics, you will learn how to participate in a traditional fin de fiesta. The focus is on developing feeling, rhythm, confidence, and above all, enjoying the shared experience of dancing together.

19:00 – 21:00 €33 / session

Discover
Flamenco

Dance, Rhythm & Culture

No experience needed — for complete beginners, and for current students who want to discover the essence of flamenco

Curious about flamenco and looking for more than a short trial class? This extended introduction offers a deeper first encounter with the art form. You will be introduced to basic flamenco dance, rhythm and palmas (hand clapping), while also gaining insight into the music and traditions behind the dance. There will be room for explanation, questions, and hands-on experience. Ideal for complete beginners and for anyone who wants to discover the essence of flamenco.

14:00 – 16:00 €33 / session All levels welcome

Your teacher

La Liz

Flamenco Teacher · Shoonya Dance Centre

With over 25 years of professional experience, Liz brings a profound and authentic knowledge of flamenco. She spent years refining her craft in Seville and Jerez under the guidance of master dancers.

As the driving force behind the company La Guardia Flamenca, she has toured the world and performed in diverse artistic contexts — from traditional flamenco to crossovers with theatre, performance art, jazz, and world music.

"I am grateful that flamenco crossed my path early in life. To me, it is a source of strength and expression, artistic creativity and lived experience. Being able to share this with others is a great joy."

In her classes, Liz focuses on developing proper technique and posture from the very first steps. She creates a space where students are encouraged to find their personal style, placing great importance on musicality and feeling.
