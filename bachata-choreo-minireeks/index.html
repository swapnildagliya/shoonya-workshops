About this series

Ready to level up your bachata?

This short choreo series is designed for dancers who've mastered the basics and crave something fresh, expressive, and unforgettable. We'll dive into a vibrant fusion of styles, blending bachata's playful soul with:

  • Elegant turns and flowing arm lines from ballroom
  • Hypnotic hip work and isolations inspired by bellydance
  • Energetic footwork drawn from diverse dance traditions

All woven into a bold, musical choreography set to a track that demands attitude, precision, and flair. Expect creativity, connection, and a touch of drama.

Your teachers

Alex & Lenka

Bachata teacher · 3× World Champion Rueda de Casino

Alex
Papaianopol

From Bucharest, Romania — 3× World Champion Rueda de Casino (2007, 2008) and Vice World Champion in Salsa (IDO 2009). 30+ years of dance experience, teaching since 2005.

Read more

Alex started dancing ballroom & other latin dances in 1993 and over the years has achieved numerous national and international awards. His teaching career for social and competition dancing started in 2005 — couples he trained became World and European Champions in Salsa, Bachata and Latino Show. He loves the expression of passion that you can share through dance.

Bachata & Raqs Sharqi teacher

Lenka
Badriyah

Silver Belly Dancer of the Universe 2012. Six years touring internationally with Bellydance Evolution, led by Jillina. Organiser of the Kan Zaman festival.

Read more

Badriyah is a professional Raqs Sharqi dancer devoted mainly to Egyptian classical and folkloric styles, modern fusion and the Golden Age of belly dance. She performed with Bellydance Evolution across Japan, Hong Kong, USA, Taiwan, Australia, Morocco, Greece and beyond. She is the organiser of Kan Zaman — The Golden Era Music & Dance Festival, and co-teaches Bachata at Shoonya alongside Alex.

Not just learning a routine — understanding the language behind it.
