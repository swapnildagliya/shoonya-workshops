About this intensive

Six classes. Two days. One deep dive into the soul of Cuban dance.

Hosted at Studio Art Deco inside Shoonya Dance Centre, Ghent's home of world dance. Cap the weekend with a social dance party Saturday evening from 21:00.

Your teachers

Wilmer

Cuba · Italy · The world's biggest Salsa stages

Wilmer & Maria are a unique and iconic dance duo in the Salsa world, combining their training in Afro-rumba, ballet, contemporary and Cuban folk dances — both graduating from Cuban dance academies. They moved from Cuba to Italy in 2002, which propelled them forward as one of the best Cuban Salsa artist duos and put them on stage at some of the biggest salsa festivals in the world. From Cuban Salsa routines and Cuban folk dance (orishas) to Afro-contemporary performances, they do it all.

Common questions

FAQ

What is Round Trip to Cuba?

A 2-day Cuban Salsa weekend intensive at Shoonya Dance Centre in Ghent, Belgium, taking place on 23–24 January 2027. Taught by internationally acclaimed duo Wilmer & Maria, it covers six 60-minute masterclasses across Afrocuban, Rumba Cubana, Son Cubano, Salsa Cubana, Timba Cubana and Afrotimba Fusión.

What level do I need to be to attend?

All levels are welcome — beginner, intermediate and advanced. The programme is designed so every participant takes something meaningful regardless of how long they've been dancing. If you can move to music, you're welcome.

Who are Wilmer & Maria?

Wilmer & Maria are a Cuban dance duo who trained at Cuban dance academies in Afro-rumba, ballet, contemporary and folk dances. They moved from Cuba to Italy in 2002 and have since performed at the world's largest Salsa congresses. Their repertoire spans Cuban Salsa, Cuban folk dance (orishas) and Afro-contemporary performance.

What's the difference between the early bird and full price?

The early-bird rate is €110 for the full weekend pass — available for a limited number of spots or until 31 July 2026, whichever comes first. After that the full price is €130. Both include access to the Saturday social night.

What is the Social Night?

After the three Saturday masterclasses, Shoonya hosts a free social dance floor from 21:00. Weekend pass holders are included. Non-workshop attendees can book in advance for €10 — limited spots also available at the door from 21:00.

Can I attend just one day?

Yes — day passes at €75 each are available from 1 August 2026, subject to remaining availability. If you want to guarantee your spot for both days, the full weekend pass is the right choice.

Where exactly is the venue?

Studio Art Deco, inside Shoonya Dance Centre at Stapelplein 41, 9000 Ghent, Belgium. The studio is Shoonya's largest and best-equipped room. More info at shoonyadance.com.

How do I register?

Everyone who registers needs a Shoonya ID — including your dance partner if joining as a couple. Don't have one yet? Create your Shoonya ID here first, then use the Register button on this page. For questions, contact us.
