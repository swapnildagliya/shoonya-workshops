About the workshop

A morning of
contemporary dance.

Spend a morning immersing yourself in contemporary dance led by Tono Ferriol, dancer and choreographer on the European scene. This 2 hour workshop will focus on improvisation and creation to help you discover the limits of your mind and body.

Dance Lab is open to everyone: whether you’re a professional dancer eager to explore new techniques or stepping into a dance studio for the very first time, everyone is invited to share their dance.

Join us on November 29th at 11:00 AM. We can’t wait to see you there!

Your teacher

Tono Ferriol

Dancer and choreographer on the European scene

Tono Ferriol trained in Oviedo and Gijón and graduated from the Real Conservatorio Profesional de Danza Mariemma in Madrid. He has danced with Europa Danse Company, the Croatian National Theatre Split, the Moravian Theatre Olomouc and the Vanemuine Estonian National Theatre, and was a guest artist with the English National Ballet. Today he works as a freelance teacher, dancer and choreographer across Europe.
