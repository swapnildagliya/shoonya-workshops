What is Kizomba?

Kizomba is a close-hold partner dance from Angola, built on smooth footwork, subtle partner communication, and the music of semba and kuduro. Sonja teaches the authentic roots of the dance alongside its social vocabulary — the steps, the connection, and the confidence to dance at a festival.

Your teacher

Sonja KikiZomba

Pioneer of Kizomba teaching in Belgium since 2006, with a Master's in Lusitanistics and a post-graduate thesis on Angola's economic and political transformation.

She has taught in 20+ countries across four continents and organised Belgium's first Kizomba Festival in 2013. A DJ since 2014, she speaks Cape Verdean Kreol and Angolan slang.

Yes. Choose one of the available modules: Module A, Module B or Module C. The registration form shows the available pass options.
